# Changelog

The version shows in the app under Budget › Settings › About. Each release is tagged `vX.Y.Z` in git.

## 1.1.0 (build 2)

- The app covers 2026 to 2035. Entries can be dated from 1 Jan 2026 to 31 Dec 2035, and Overview, History and the Year view browse that range.
- New: Export to Excel (Settings, or the button at the bottom of the Year view). The .xlsx file has a dashboard with charts, a month-by-month sheet, every transaction, and goals and debts.

## 1.0.0 (build 1)

- First version: Overview, History, Add, Budget, Goals, Debts, Year view, Settings, onboarding, app lock, backups and CSV export.
