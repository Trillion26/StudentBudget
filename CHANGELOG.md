# Changelog

The version shows in the app under Budget › Settings › About. Each release is tagged `vX.Y.Z` in git.

## 2.1.0 (build 4)

- The app is now called **Veen Budget**, with the piggy-bank icon and colours to match it: forest green, cream and gold.
- Exports are named `veen-budget-2026.xlsx` and `veen-budget-2026.csv`.

## 2.0.0 (build 3)

The app is now a household budget for a Dutch couple with children and a mortgage.

- Amounts are in euros, written and typed the Dutch way: € 1.250,50.
- New household categories: housing and utilities, health insurance per partner, children and childcare, transport, and more. Budgets start at € 0.
- Who paid: each entry can be Joint or one of the two partners. Overview, History, the CSV and the Excel export show the split. The names are asked when you start, and can be changed in Settings.
- Mortgage: add each loan part (annuity, linear or interest-only) to see what you still owe, this month's interest and repayment, the interest paid each year for your tax return, and a reminder a year before the fixed rate ends.
- Debts are now called Loans.
- The Excel export has who paid, mortgage interest on the dashboard, and a mortgage schedule month by month.
- The CSV export uses semicolons and decimal commas, for Excel with Dutch settings.
- Backups from version 1 still restore.

## 1.1.0 (build 2)

- The app covers 2026 to 2035. Entries can be dated from 1 Jan 2026 to 31 Dec 2035, and Overview, History and the Year view browse that range.
- The app is now called Emily’s Budget (Em’s Budget on the home screen). Backups from earlier versions still restore.
- New look: the piggy-bank app icon, and teal, gold, green and blue colours to match it, in light and dark mode.
- New: Export to Excel (Settings, or the button at the bottom of the Year view). The .xlsx file has a dashboard with charts, a month-by-month sheet, every transaction, and goals and debts.

## 1.0.0 (build 1)

- First version: Overview, History, Add, Budget, Goals, Debts, Year view, Settings, onboarding, app lock, backups and CSV export.
