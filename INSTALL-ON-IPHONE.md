# Install Veen Budget on your iPhone

Veen Budget isn't in the App Store. You install it yourself with a free tool and your own Apple ID. It takes about 20–30 minutes the first time, and you need a **Windows PC or a Mac** for the first setup only.

**What you need**

- An iPhone with iOS 15 or newer
- Your Apple ID (the one you use for the App Store) and its password
- A Windows PC or Mac, and your iPhone's cable
- The app file, **StudentBudget.ipa**, from the link your friend sent you

**Two things to know first**

1. **The app has to be refreshed every 7 days.** That's Apple's rule for apps installed this way. If you forget, the app won't open until you refresh it. Your data is still there when you do. Set a weekly reminder now (step 6).
2. **Save a backup now and then.** Your budget only lives on your phone. In the app, go to **Budget › Settings › Save backup** and save it to iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Do this every week or two, and always before reinstalling the app.

There are two free tools. Pick **one**:

- **SideStore (recommended).** After setup you refresh on the phone itself, with no computer needed.
- **AltStore Classic.** It refreshes automatically when your phone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi.

---

## Option A: SideStore (recommended)

### 1. On your computer

1. On Windows, install **iTunes** from the Microsoft Store or from apple.com.
2. Download **iloader**, the SideStore installer, from <https://sidestore.io>. Follow the link to iloader and pick the Windows `.exe` or `.msi`. Install it.
3. Plug your iPhone into the computer with the cable. Unlock it, and tap **Trust** when it asks whether to trust this computer.
4. Open **iloader**, sign in with your Apple ID (type it exactly, capitals matter), select your iPhone, and choose **Install SideStore (Stable)**.

### 2. On your iPhone

1. Open the **App Store** and install **LocalDevVPN** (free).
2. Go to **Settings › General › VPN & Device Management**. Under **Developer App**, tap your Apple ID, then tap **Trust** and confirm.
3. iOS 16 or newer: go to **Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode**, turn it **on** and let the phone restart. After the restart, tap **Turn On** when asked.
4. Open **LocalDevVPN** and tap **Connect**. If it asks to allow VPN configurations, tap **Allow**.
5. Open **SideStore** and sign in with the same Apple ID.

### 3. Install Veen Budget

1. On your iPhone, open the link your friend sent in **Safari**. Tap **StudentBudget.ipa** and then **Download**. The file goes to **Files › Downloads**.
2. Make sure LocalDevVPN is connected.
3. Open **SideStore** › **My Apps** › the **+** button (top left). Pick **StudentBudget.ipa** from Downloads.
4. Wait for it to finish. **Veen Budget** appears on your home screen.

### 4. Every week: refresh

Open **LocalDevVPN** and tap **Connect**. Then open **SideStore** › **My Apps** › **Refresh All**. No computer needed.

## Option B: AltStore Classic

### 1. On your Windows PC

1. Install **iTunes** and **iCloud** from **apple.com**. The AltStore website links to the right versions, which are not the Microsoft Store ones.
2. Download **AltServer for Windows** from <https://altstore.io> and install it. Open it. It sits as a small diamond icon near the clock.
3. Plug in your iPhone, unlock it and tap **Trust**.
4. Open iTunes, click the phone icon, tick **Sync with this iPhone over Wi-Fi** and click **Apply**.
5. Click the AltServer icon near the clock › **Install AltStore** › your iPhone. Sign in with your Apple ID.

### 2. On your iPhone

1. Go to **Settings › General › VPN & Device Management**, tap your Apple ID and tap **Trust**.
2. iOS 16 or newer: go to **Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode**, turn it **on** and restart.

### 3. Install Veen Budget

1. Open the link your friend sent in **Safari** and download **StudentBudget.ipa**. It goes to Files › Downloads.
2. Open **AltStore** › **My Apps** › **+** (top left) › pick **StudentBudget.ipa**.

### 4. Every week: refresh

Leave your PC on with **AltServer** running, and keep your phone on the **same Wi-Fi**. AltStore then refreshes by itself. To do it by hand, open **AltStore** › **My Apps** › **Refresh All**.

---

## 5. First time in the app

- Three short screens ask what money comes in each month and when your money month starts. You can skip them and change everything later.
- Tap the gold **+** to log what you spend or receive.
- Your data never leaves your phone unless you save a backup or export it yourself.

## 6. Set a weekly reminder

Open **Reminders** › **New Reminder** › "Refresh Veen Budget" › tap **i** › set a date › **Repeat › Weekly**.

## Good to know

- **A free Apple ID can run at most 3 sideloaded apps at once.** SideStore or AltStore counts as one of them.
- **Getting a new version:** download the new `.ipa` and install it the same way, using **+** in My Apps. It replaces the old version and keeps your data, as long as you use the same tool and the same Apple ID. **Save a backup first, just in case.**
- **If the app ever loses your data** (for example after reinstalling with a different tool or Apple ID): open **Budget › Settings › Restore backup** and pick your latest backup file.
- **"Unable to verify app" or the app won't open:** it has gone more than 7 days without a refresh. Refresh it (step 4) and it opens again with your data.
- **"Untrusted Developer":** do the **Trust** step again under Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.
- **Face ID lock:** in Budget › Settings, turn on **App lock**.
